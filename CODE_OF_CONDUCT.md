# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes technical disagreement and direct engineering critique. Participation must still remain professional and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss designs, evidence, measurements, tests, and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- give contributors room to correct mistakes;
- distinguish safety-critical criticism from personal criticism;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending commercial products or services;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Engineering discussion

Strong disagreement is acceptable. Claims about dimensions, safety, load paths, electrical design, licensing, or manufacturability should be supported with measurements, calculations, drawings, tests, or reliable references when practical.

Safety concerns may be escalated even when they delay a feature or release.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation by people who repeatedly violate this policy.

Good-faith technical criticism, bug reports, competing designs, and documentation of project defects are not misconduct.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-managed spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official collaboration channels.
