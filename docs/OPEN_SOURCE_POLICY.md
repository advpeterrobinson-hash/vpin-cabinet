# Open-Source Policy

## Licence

The official Virtual Pinball Cabinet project is released under the **CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 — Strongly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-S-2.0)**.

The definitive legal terms are in the repository's `LICENSE` file.

Official Source Location:

https://github.com/advpeterrobinson-hash/vpin-cabinet

## What this means

You may study, copy, modify, manufacture, use, and commercially sell products based on the project, subject to the licence.

The project intentionally permits commercial use. Open source does not mean that a fabricated cabinet, kit, consulting service, installation, or support service must be provided at zero price.

The strong-reciprocal requirement is about keeping the **design/source open**.

When you convey modified Covered Source or a Product based on it, CERN-OHL-S-2.0 requires the applicable Complete Source to be provided or made available from a Source Location, requires modification/source-location notices where applicable, and requires modified Covered Source to remain under the same reciprocal licence.

Under the strongly reciprocal variant, incorporating Covered Source into a larger design can make that larger work modified Covered Source, subject to the licence's definitions and exceptions for Available Components.

## What "free" means here

The project's requirement is **source freedom and reciprocity**, not zero-price commerce.

A fabricator or company may charge for physical cabinets, kits, parts, labor, integration, support, or other services. When the licence's Conveyance conditions are triggered, the applicable Complete Source and modifications must be made available under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 as required by the licence.

Private experimentation that is not Conveyed does not create a project-specific publication requirement beyond the actual CERN-OHL-S-2.0 terms.

## Required project attribution

The project's `NOTICE.md` specifies the original official Source Location and the manner in which it must be displayed for Products based on the project.

The original project link must remain with applicable Notices:

https://github.com/advpeterrobinson-hash/vpin-cabinet

A modified project should additionally identify the Source Location for its own modified Complete Source.

## Improvements and forks

Forks are welcome.

If a fork or commercial derivative is conveyed, improvements to Covered Source cannot simply be closed and withheld contrary to the reciprocal terms. The modified source must remain available under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 as required by the licence.

Private experimentation that is never conveyed is treated according to the actual licence text; this policy does not attempt to add restrictions beyond CERN-OHL-S-2.0.

## Contributions back upstream

Contributors are encouraged, but not forced by project process, to send useful changes back to the official repository as pull requests.

The legal reciprocal obligations are governed by `LICENSE`; contributing specifically to the upstream repository is not a substitute for satisfying those obligations.

## Scope

Unless a file states otherwise, CERN-OHL-S-2.0 applies to original project source in this repository, including hardware-design material, CAD source, drawings, configuration/design data, build/generation scripts, validation code, BOMs, and project documentation.

Third-party material is not relicensed. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
