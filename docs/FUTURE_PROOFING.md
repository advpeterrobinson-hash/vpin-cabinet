# Future-proofing policy

## Owner direction

The cabinet is expected to last much longer than its televisions, PC, controllers, amplifiers, and power electronics. Exact historical Williams dimensions are therefore secondary to long-term serviceability and replacement-part compatibility.

The owner explicitly accepts approximately 10–50 mm dimensional deviations when they materially improve the design.

## Permanent structure vs replaceable electronics

Permanent wood and metal structure should be sized around service envelopes and modular interfaces. Electronics-specific geometry belongs in replaceable parts where practical.

Preferred pattern:

- permanent shell: generous service envelope;
- replaceable carrier/adapter: actual VESA or chassis pattern;
- replaceable bezel/filler: closes cosmetic gaps;
- slotted mounting where practical;
- cable service loops and accessible connectors;
- no trapped electronics behind irreversible joinery.

## Backbox

The authentic reference backbox is 730.25 mm wide. This is too restrictive for many modern 31.5–32 inch displays.

v0.6 therefore targets 780 mm outer width and a 740 x 450 x 100 mm monitor service envelope. This is a deliberate +49.75 mm deviation from the reference width and remains within the owner-approved future-proofing allowance.

The exact backglass monitor is no longer required before cutting the permanent backbox shell. Only its replaceable monitor carrier and cosmetic bezel depend on the exact purchased model.

## Main playfield cabinet

The current 558.8 mm Williams reference body fits the selected LG OLED42C5 but leaves little cross-width growth margin once an 8 mm side skin is preserved.

Before main-cabinet dimensions are frozen, evaluate a modest width increase against:

- future 42-inch-class display chassis widths;
- desired minimum side skin;
- playfield glass sourcing;
- lockdown-bar sourcing/fabrication;
- siderail sourcing/fabrication;
- hinge/cradle width;
- flipper/button ergonomics;
- Williams visual proportions.

A likely evaluation range is roughly 570–575 mm external body width, but no new body width is approved until hardware consequences are checked parametrically.

## PC and electronics

The existing open-frame ATX chassis is an initial component, not a permanent dimensional constraint. The drawer uses a service envelope and a replaceable chassis adapter.

Controller boards, amplifiers, power supplies, and DOF boards should mount to removable grid/slotted panels or DIN-style subassemblies where appropriate so future boards can change size without cabinet surgery.

## Manufacturing rule

Do not spend structural margin merely to reproduce a historical dimension when 10–50 mm of additional envelope materially reduces future replacement risk.
