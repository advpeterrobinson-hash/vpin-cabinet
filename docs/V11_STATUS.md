# v0.11 status

Electrical/cooling/cable-routing architecture is defined for packaging and CNC planning.

This stage is intentionally not manufacturing-ready. Exact PSU wattage/current, wire gauges, fuse values, connector families, output-controller boards, relay/MOSFET hardware, fan model, thermostat model, toy voltages and toy hardware remain BOM-stage selections that must be validated against measured current and vendor documentation.

The purpose of v0.11 is to reserve the physical infrastructure early enough that the cabinet does not require destructive drilling or improvised cable routing later.
