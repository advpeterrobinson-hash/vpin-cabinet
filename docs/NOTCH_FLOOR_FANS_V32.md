## Conservative analytical thermal gate

Gross intake and gross rear exhaust aperture areas are equal: 2π58² = 21,136.635 mm² each. This is neither net guard/filter area nor a fan flow rating. The Ø116 floor apertures are unobstructed; the immediate 25 mm discharge columns above both fans have 0% modeled obstruction. S2 is 119 mm above the fan tops and covers 55.481% of each rotor's projected area. This overhead restriction must be included in later airflow/system-impedance analysis.

An 8 mm radius diagnostic route from each intake passes around the low PC and under S3, then rises behind the playfield toward the two rear fans. Exact CAD solids clear both routes. These are open-volume airflow indicators, not ducts. Direct geometric shortcuts meet PC/S3 geometry; intake/exhaust separation and different mounting planes expose useful internal volume rather than an obvious adjacent short circuit. This does not establish actual flow distribution, PC hotspot control or adequate cooling.

No physical thermal prototype is planned. Thermal adequacy is NOT certified. Before manufacturing, an analytical gate must combine selected fans' published airflow/static-pressure curves and noise, filter/guard pressure drop and net area, conservative expected PC/GPU heat, PSU losses, amplifier/audio load, and warm Brazilian ambient conditions. Screen 35°C ambient and a 40°C sensitivity case as provisional design scenarios; replace these with the owner's actual site requirement. Heat loads and allowable component/air temperature rises are still inputs to freeze. Use heat balance Q ≥ P/(ρ·cp·ΔT), fan operating points against the combined system resistance, and a defensible distribution/recirculation analysis; geometry or four fans alone cannot close this gate.
