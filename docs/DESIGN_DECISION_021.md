# DEC-021 — Classic legs + PinSkates + top-access lift-out PC service

**Status:** owner accepted direction from visual/reference review, 2026-09-15.

This decision supersedes the v0.20 assumptions for leg-corner woodworking, integrated/retractable mobility hardware and forward-travel PC drawer service.

## Legs

Keep classic pinball legs and normal levelers. Do not consume large cabinet interior volumes with permanent 18+18 mm plywood corner blocks by default.

Use compact formed-steel internal leg brackets with through-bolted hardware and local steel spreader/backing plates. The exact hardware and bolt pattern are measured from the actual selected classic leg/bracket set before CNC release.

Compact local plywood backing may be added only if physical proof testing shows unacceptable plywood bearing/crush or cabinet-joint movement.

## Mobility

Use external removable PinSkates-style assemblies to move the machine.

There are no built-in casters, retractable wheels, caster cutouts or permanent wheel mechanisms in the cabinet. The skates are temporary equipment, removed before play, so the cabinet plays entirely on its classic legs/levelers.

## PC service

Do not use a long forward-travel drawer toward the cabinet front.

Mount the open-frame PC on a removable internal lift-out sled. Normal service occurs from above after the playfield is raised and positively supported. The sled releases from compact captive/quick-retainer points and lifts vertically through the playfield opening. A quick-disconnect service harness is required.

This keeps the cabinet front intact, reduces moving parts, preserves interior space for DOF/audio/electronics, and better matches the service philosophy visible in the owner-provided reference cabinets.

## Internal-layout principle

Treat the reference photos as architecture guidance, not dimensional templates:

- preserve a large open central service volume;
- prefer localized removable electronics boards/rails to broad fixed furniture;
- keep PC/power/controllers accessible from above;
- preserve SSF sidewall zones;
- keep hardware removable without destructive cabinet work.
