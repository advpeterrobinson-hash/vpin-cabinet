# Licensing FAQ

This page is a plain-language guide to the project's licensing policy. It does not replace the legal text in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

## What licence does the project use?

Original project material is released under the **CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 — Strongly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-S-2.0)** unless a file explicitly states otherwise.

Official project / Source Location:

**https://github.com/advpeterrobinson-hash/vpin-cabinet**

See also [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Can I build one for myself?

Yes. You may study, modify, and make Products from the Covered Source subject to the licence.

## Can I sell cabinets, kits, parts, or services?

Yes. Commercial use is allowed.

You may charge for fabricated cabinets, flat-pack kits, parts, assembly, installation, support, consulting, or related services.

The reciprocal obligation concerns the design/source. When you Convey modified Covered Source or Products based on it, the applicable Complete Source must be provided or made available under the licence as required by CERN-OHL-S-2.0.

## Do I have to publish every private experiment?

No project-specific rule requires publication of changes that remain private and are never Conveyed. The actual CERN-OHL-S-2.0 text controls.

If you later Convey a Product based on modified Covered Source, the licence's source-availability and Notice obligations apply.

## If I improve the design and sell the improved version, can I keep the improvements proprietary?

Not if doing so would conflict with the strongly reciprocal licence.

CERN-OHL-S-2.0 requires modified Covered Source to remain under the same licence when conveyed, and Products must be accompanied by Complete Source or a Source Location as specified by the licence.

## Must the source be available at zero price?

The licence requires source availability under its terms; it does not require physical Products or services to be sold at zero price.

This project's phrase "keep improvements free" means **keep the design/source open and reusable under the same reciprocal licence**.

## Do I have to link the official project?

Preserve the project Notices.

The project's Source Location Notice identifies the official project:

**https://github.com/advpeterrobinson-hash/vpin-cabinet**

[NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) specifies the attribution wording and how the Source Location is displayed for Products.

A modified fork should also identify the Source Location for its own modified Complete Source where required by the licence.

## Do I have to submit my changes back to the official repository?

A pull request back upstream is strongly encouraged because it helps everyone share improvements, but upstream contribution is not itself the legal test.

The legal obligations are the ones in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

## Can I create a fork?

Yes. Forks and alternative implementations are welcome, subject to the licence and Notices.

Please make it clear when a fork is not the official project, and do not imply endorsement by the original maintainers.

## What about third-party parts, manuals, artwork, ROMs, or trademarks?

They keep their original rights and licences.

The project's licence does not magically relicense third-party content. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Do not contribute proprietary CAD, copyrighted manuals, game assets, ROMs, artwork, or other restricted material unless redistribution rights are clear.

## What licence applies to contributions?

By submitting material to the official repository, contributors confirm that they have the right to submit it and provide it under the project's applicable licence. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Is the design already safe or manufacturing-ready because it is open source?

No.

Public availability is not a certification, conformity assessment, engineering approval, or manufacturing release. Follow the project's hardware-freeze gates, proof tests, warnings, and release status.
