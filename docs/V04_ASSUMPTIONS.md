# v0.4 assumptions requiring later replacement

1. OLED/front longitudinal placement remains the 44.4 mm reference-derived setback.
2. Glass clearance is provisionally 12 mm normal to the playfield plane.
3. Cradle mass is estimated at 3.0 kg.
4. Combined OLED+cradle CG is approximated at the OLED longitudinal midpoint.
5. Cradle rail sections are only 20 x 20 mm packaging envelopes; alloy/wall thickness not selected.
6. Hinge axis is provisionally 35 mm behind the OLED rear edge.
7. Gas-strut attachment coordinates and 300 N candidate force are optimization seeds, not purchasing values.
8. Exact LG VESA-hole offsets from chassis edges are not assumed.
9. Backbox collision geometry is not yet part of the generated master and is a v0.5 validation requirement.
10. OLED side-wall pocket geometry remains uncut in this stage; the previously validated 9.6 mm nominal pocket requirement is a packaging value only.
