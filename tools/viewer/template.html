Current scope

One flat plywood base, one wood dowel, two open CNC wood cradles, four commercial straps, eight strap screws and six interior support screws. SERVICE: raise the front; the dowel rotates in the cradles. LIFT-OUT: start from PLAY without glass, lift 48 mm to remove both ends vertically; base, TV, VESA, dowel and straps leave together. Remove glass and release lockdown before opening. Supports remain screwed to the sides during removal. Vertical load bears directly on the floor. Glue optional after validation; nails not used.
