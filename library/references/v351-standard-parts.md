# V35.1 commercial references

Reviewed 2026-10-06. Original functional analysis only; no vendor CAD, images or manufacturing drawings imported. This supersedes V35 study policy:5mm local tempered glass is primary, siderails optional, commercial rear channel screwed. Reference envelopes are not purchased measurements.

- [Tukkari premium assembly guide](https://www.tukkari.com/advisor/assembly-guide-widebody-vpin-cabinet-premium-flat-pack): shell assembly, parallel playfield holder, glass channels followed by optional siderails. Public rear-channel illustration shows ordinary screw fixing; its screw count/dimensions are not adopted. Our one-face CNC, protected supports and removable shelves remain owner-authorized differences.
- [Tukkari component guide](https://tukkaricz.s2.cdn-upgates.com/6/669767d6279c87-cabinet-parts-usa-ver-01-2026.pdf): commercial ecosystem reference, not drilling authority.
- [Widebody A-16055](https://www.pinballspareparts.com.au/a-16055.html): A-17996 widebody class and matching A-16773-1/A-9174-4 receiver references. Actual engagement and width must be measured.
- [A-16773-1 receiver](https://www.pinballlife.com/williamsbally-lockdown-bar-lever-guide-receiver-assembly-wpcwpc-95.html): published family compatibility. No reliable dimensioned latch trajectory found. The modeled520×42×40mm volume is available-space planning, not a manufacturer dimension. Purchase gate includes withdrawal, latch sweep, fastener access and mounting.
- [Rear channel03-8091-2](https://www.pinballlife.com/widebody-playfield-glass-rear-plastic-channel.html): nominal22-15/16in length. Actual profile/flange/holes remain unknown. Original U packaging model supports screwed architecture (added forward flange rejected for matrix interference); confirm selected part before routing/pilots.
- [Side polymer channel](https://www.pinballlife.com/playfield-glass-side-rail-plastic-channel.html):03-7135-1 reference family. Final SKU must accept actual5mm glass; no assumption that a3/16in channel accepts it.
- [Optional WPC siderails](https://www.pinballlife.com/47-316-williamsbally-wpc-stainless-steel-side-rails-set-of-2.html): long reference SKU may require verified rear trimming. Cabinet and glass work with zero rails; no new custom rail.

Legs, bracket, shooter, coin-door and representative thin42/43-inch TV sources remain documented in [V35 study sources](v35-standard-parts.md). These references do not select purchased hardware or release machining. Prior3/16in glass and mandatory integral-rail studies are superseded.
