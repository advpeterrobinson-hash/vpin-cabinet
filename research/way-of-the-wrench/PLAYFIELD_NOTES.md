# Playfield-related research notes

This file is intentionally concise. The Way of the Wrench playlists are research references, not dimensional authorities for this cabinet.

For the playfield-service subsystem, the useful design themes are:

- service access must be planned before filling the cabinet with electronics and feedback toys;
- removable/accessible mounting is preferable to burying hardware under the display;
- high-end virtual-pinball builds accumulate substantial wiring, controllers, lighting, and feedback hardware, so the raised playfield must expose a deliberate service volume rather than merely lift a screen;
- wiring should be labeled and routed with disconnection/service in mind;
- force-feedback and SSF locations should be reserved early because later shelves and braces can compromise them.

Our design intentionally goes beyond the reference builds by requiring a structural LG C5 cradle, dual gas struts, an independent positive mechanical safety support, and a slide-out PC module. No hinge/strut dimension is copied from the videos without independent engineering validation.
